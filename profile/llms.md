## Why Griddo?

Griddo is a GEO-optimized DXP built to deliver content that AI engines discover and cite — and that AI agents can act on. With a native MCP server, your agents can read, write, and publish directly inside Griddo, extending the author experience beyond the UI and into your own workflows.

Purpose-made for universities and educational institutions seeking a modern, user-friendly platform to manage multiple websites, landing pages, and events. Griddo's no-code interface, AI-driven tools, and modular design make it ideal for cross-functional teams — marketing, content, communications, and research — without requiring programming expertise.

Additionally, Griddo is the only DXP with a fully brand-tailored Design System at its heart, guaranteeing that all content created with Griddo adheres to the university's visual identity.

## Key Features

- **No-Code Interface**: Build and manage pages with a drag-and-drop builder, instant previews at any device resolution, and no coding required.
- **Modular Design System**: Create flexible, responsive layouts with reusable modules, ensuring brand consistency across all sites and landing pages.
- **AI-Powered Tools**:
  - Automatic image tagging
  - SEO meta tag generation
  - Multilingual translations
  - Content personalization with Griddo Personal Experience (GPX)
- **Multi-Site Management**: Centralize control of multiple websites, landing pages, news, events, and faculty profiles with content reuse capabilities. Structure all your marketing campaigns in one interface.
- **MACH Architecture**:
  - **Microservices:** Independent services that manage internal and external integrations across your institution
  - **API-first:** A RESTful API combined with internal GraphQL handles content delivery and service integration
  - **Cloud-native:** Fully managed SaaS on AWS — secure, scalable, and always up to date
  - **Headless:** Use Griddo as your content hub regardless of your front end
  - **MCP Server:** Connect your AI agents to Griddo and run reports, analysis, and bulk tasks directly from your workflow
- **Cloud Native Infrastructure**:
  - Serverless deployment for enhanced security and reliability
  - Reduced maintenance costs
  - Increased scalability
  - Edge performance
- **SEO and GEO Performance**: Generates bot-readable static HTML, optimizes load times, and achieves top Core Web Vitals scores. Content architecture optimized for AI search engine discovery and citation (GEO).
- **Role-Based Collaboration**: Assign permissions for SEO, analytics, content, and design teams, with traceability for external collaborators.
- **Digital Asset Management**: Griddo Images optimizes media handling for efficient content workflows.
- **Integrations**: Connect with any system. Data stays in sync, everywhere.
  Griddo's API-first architecture connects to any system your institution already runs — SIS, CRM, LMS, analytics platforms, and external data sources. RESTful and GraphQL APIs handle bi-directional data flow, so a change in one system propagates across your entire digital ecosystem automatically. One unified data layer. Every team working from the same source of truth.
  - **CRM:** Salesforce, Zoho CRM, MS Dynamics
  - **LMS:** Moodle, Canvas
  - **Analytics:** Google Analytics, Hotjar
  - **University Systems:** SIS, ERP

## How It Works

1. **Design**: Use the intuitive drag-and-drop builder to create pages with modular components, ensuring 100% responsive designs.
2. **Manage**: Centralize content (news, events, faculty profiles) across multiple sites with reusable modules.
3. **Enhance**: Use AI tools for automated image tagging, translations, and SEO optimization.
4. **Collaborate**: Assign roles to marketing, content, and design teams for seamless teamwork.
5. **Publish**: Deploy content instantly with real-time previews, optimized for performance and accessibility.

**Technical Details**: Griddo is built on TypeScript, Node.js, and React, using a serverless cloud infrastructure for high performance and scalability.
